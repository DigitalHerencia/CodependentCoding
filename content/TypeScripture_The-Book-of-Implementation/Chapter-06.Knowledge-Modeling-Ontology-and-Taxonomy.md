**The Book of Implementation™**

## Canonical realization

- Keep TypeScripture physically simple: two flat books plus chapter maps.

- A canonical artifact needs only metadata that changes authority, retrieval, ownership, lifecycle, or automation. Do not preserve historical source frontmatter in the final chapter body.

## Validation
