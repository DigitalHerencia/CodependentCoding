# Worked example: GreenRoute (crm preset)

A dry run of `/idea` on `hipster-stack new greenroute --preset crm --color "#2f7a3d"`. Twelve questions, each a proposal the owner confirmed or corrected.

| #   | Proposal as asked, then the owner's answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | "Small landscaping companies lose a morning a week because scheduling lives in text messages; today they cope with a whiteboard and group chats." Yes, add missed recurring visits and late billing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 2   | "Users: owner-operator (owner), office manager (admin), crew lead (member). The owner pays." Add a bookkeeper as viewer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 3   | "The app keeps track of clients, properties, jobs and visits." Add crews.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 4   | "property `name:string, street:string, city:string, gateCode:string?, notes:text?, accountId:ref(CrmAccount)`; crew `name:string, leadId:ref(User)?`; job `name, kind:enum(...), recurrence:enum(...), price:decimal?, propertyId:ref(Property)`; visit `scheduledAt:datetime, status:enum(planned\|done\|skipped), completedAt:datetime?, completionNote:text?, jobId:ref(Job), crewId:ref(Crew)`." Yes. Clients: the installed crm `CrmAccount`, no new entity. |
| 5   | "v1 slices: property, job, crew, visit, visit completion." Yes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 6   | "v1 will not do invoicing, route optimization or a client portal." Also no offline mode, no payroll.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 7   | "Crew lead opens today's visits, reads the gate code, taps Done with a note." Yes; plus booking a recurring job.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 8   | "v1 works if a crew lead marks a visit done in under 30 s." Add: four weeks booked in under 2 min, completed visits visible the same day.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 9   | "Keep crm, add nothing; new domain `field` for property, crew, job, visit." Yes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 10  | "Admins manage everything but billing; members read jobs and complete their crew's visits." Members must not see prices.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 11  | "Practical and calm, for crew leads outdoors and an office manager at a desk." Yes; high contrast on the visits list.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 12  | "No integrations; visits list under 2 s on a phone; keep history three years; you use zsh." Yes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

Recorded: DEC-002 product definition, DEC-003 clients are crm accounts and `field` is a new domain, DEC-004 (proposed) how recurring visits get created.

Handed back:

```text
hipster-stack slice property --module field --label "Field" --icon building --plural properties --fields "name:string, street:string, city:string, gateCode:string?, notes:text?, accountId:ref(CrmAccount)"
hipster-stack slice crew --module field --fields "name:string, leadId:ref(User)?"
hipster-stack slice job --module field --fields "name:string, kind:enum(mowing|cleanup|install|irrigation), recurrence:enum(once|weekly|biweekly|monthly), price:decimal?, propertyId:ref(Property)"
hipster-stack slice visit --module field --fields "scheduledAt:datetime, status:enum(planned|done|skipped), completedAt:datetime?, completionNote:text?, jobId:ref(Job), crewId:ref(Crew)"
```

Order matters: a `ref(Model)` target must exist before the slice that points at it.
