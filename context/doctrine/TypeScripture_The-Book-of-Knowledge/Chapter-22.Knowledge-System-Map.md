# Chapter 22: Knowledge System Map

**The Book of Knowledge™**

## Canonical hierarchy

- TypeScripture™ Canonical Doctrine → Codependent Coding™ engineering doctrine/governance → Loaded Vibes™ WebApp Architecture → Hipster Stack™ TechStack → product/reference implementations.

## Book relationship

- The Book of Knowledge defines what concepts mean, who owns decisions, which relationships and invariants exist, and what evidence can justify claims.
- The Book of Implementation defines how those concepts are realized concretely in files, interfaces, schemas, code, workflows, configuration, tests, and runtime boundaries.

## Runtime truth ownership

- Clerk: external identity/session truth.
- PostgreSQL application model: local user/membership/RBAC/domain/entitlement truth.
- Stripe: provider payment/settlement truth.
- Domain policy + Workflow: legal product transitions.
- Application authz + PostgreSQL RLS: intended access + containment.
- Next.js app boundary: URL/HTTP/framework outcomes.
- Feature: page experience orchestration.
- Tests/builds/review/runtime observation: conformance evidence, not underlying product truth.

## Rule

- Reference implementations supply evidence. They do not become doctrine merely because they are complete or older documentation called them “Golden.”
